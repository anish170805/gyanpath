# Table of Contents Instructions

## Required format
Use a three-column table:

| Chapter / Section | Page No. |
|---|---:|

In the final Word report, preserve the source's two-column conceptual layout:
- `Chapter / Section`
- `Page No.`

## Include
- All front matter items.
- Every chapter.
- Every numbered section and subsection that actually exists in the final report.
- References.
- Bibliography.

## Numbering rules
- Chapter: `Chapter 1: Introduction`
- Main section: `1.1 Introduction`
- Nested section: `1.3.1 <Title>`
- Use the same numbering as the chapter files.
- Do not include sections that were planned but not written.
