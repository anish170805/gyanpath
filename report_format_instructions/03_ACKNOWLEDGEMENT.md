# Acknowledgement Instructions

## Required structure
- Heading: `ACKNOWLEDGEMENT`
- Write in first person.
- Start with submission of the internship/vocational training report and degree fulfillment context.
- Thank, in this order where applicable:
  1. Principal / institution leadership
  2. Head of Department
  3. Faculty mentor / guide
  4. Department faculty and staff
  5. Parents, family, friends, and others who helped
- End with student identification fields.

## Template structure

```text
ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

[Opening paragraph about submitting the report and taking the opportunity to thank
everyone directly or indirectly related to the project/vocational training.]

[Paragraph thanking the Principal and Head of Department.]

[Paragraph thanking the mentor/guide, faculty, and departmental staff.]

[Paragraph thanking parents, family, friends, and others.]

Name of the Student: <STUDENT NAME>
Roll No.: <ROLL NUMBER>
Enrollment No.: <ENROLLMENT NUMBER>
```

## Project-folder instruction
Use names and designations only from `<PROJECT_ROOT>/project-source/`. If a name is not available, keep a placeholder such as `<MENTOR NAME>` rather than guessing.
