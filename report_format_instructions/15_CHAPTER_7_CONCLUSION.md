# CHAPTER 7 — CONCLUSION

## Heading format

```text
CHAPTER 7
CONCLUSION
```

## Required section
- `7.1 Conclusion`

## What to write
Summarize:
1. the original problem,
2. the proposed solution,
3. what was implemented,
4. key technical components,
5. major outcomes,
6. limitations where relevant,
7. overall contribution.

Do not introduce new modules, results, or claims here.

## Writing pattern
The conclusion should read as a connected final assessment, not a repetition of every chapter.

## Project-folder instruction
Use only verified project facts from `<PROJECT_ROOT>/project-source/` and the completed report chapters.
