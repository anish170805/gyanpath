# Declaration Instructions

## Required structure
- Heading: `DECLARATION`
- Write one formal declaration paragraph stating:
  - the project/report title,
  - that the work was carried out during vocational training,
  - that the statements and conclusions are based on the student's own work,
  - that the work has not been submitted elsewhere for another degree/diploma/certificate.
- Leave signature space.
- Add student name, roll number, and enrollment number.

## Template

```text
DECLARATION

We the undersigned solemnly declare that the report of the project work entitled
"<PROJECT TITLE>" is based on my own work carried out during the course of my
vocational training.

I assert that the statements made and conclusions drawn are an outcome of the
project work. I further declare that, to the best of my knowledge and belief, this
report does not contain any part of any work that has been submitted for the award
of any other degree, diploma, or certificate in this University or any other
University of India or any other country.


------------------------------
(Signature of Student)

Name of the Student: <STUDENT NAME>
Roll No.: <ROLL NUMBER>
Enrollment No.: <ENROLLMENT NUMBER>
```

## Project-folder instruction
Take all student and project identifiers from `<PROJECT_ROOT>/project-source/`.
Do not change the declaration into a technical section.
